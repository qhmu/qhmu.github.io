Anqiang Ye is a PhD student in the Mu Lab starting from Sept 2026. He obtained his bachelor’s degree in Biopharmaceutical from China Pharmaceutical University, followed by a master’s degree in Microbial and Biochemical Pharmacy from Wuhan University. Prior to joining the Mu Lab, he worked on the construction of microbial metabolic network models and the application of large language models in synthetic biology. For his PhD studies, he is focusing on the multi-omics study of bone tumors and application of AI in the medical field.
